03 — How I'd work as your PM

Governance built into the product, not bolted on at the end.

The same loop I've run as an analyst, now owned end to end as a product manager.

Step 1

Understand the risk

Map regulations, policies and pain points with risk, compliance and business owners before writing a single story.

Step 2

Turn it into requirements

Write specs, flows and acceptance criteria where every control traces back to the rule it satisfies.

Step 3

Release with control

UAT sign-off, deployment and rollback runbooks, clear owners — so every change is safe and auditable.

Step 4

Monitor & improve

Dashboards that show control health and adoption, feeding the next iteration of the roadmap.
